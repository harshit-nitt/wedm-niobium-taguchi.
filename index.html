Which Wire-EDM settings give pure niobium the smoothest surface?

A Taguchi L9 analysis of how pulse-on time, pulse-off time and peak current affect surface roughness (Ra), across nine trials with three readings each. Developed during a research internship at the Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre, Thiruvananthapuram, May to July 2026.

    1

    Experiment design

    Three factors at three levels would need 27 trials as a full factorial. The L9 orthogonal array covers the main effects in 9, with every level of every factor appearing exactly three times.

    Factors and levels

    Levels coded 1 to 3 in the array.

    L9 orthogonal array

    Coded levels with the actual settings used in each run.

    2

    Dataset

    Three Ra readings per run. The mean feeds the ANOVA; the spread between readings feeds the signal-to-noise ratio.

    Ra readings by run

    Dots are individual readings, the bar is the run mean. The lowest-roughness run is highlighted.

    Run summary

    Smaller-the-better S/N = −10 log₁₀(mean of Ra²). Higher S/N means lower and more consistent roughness.

    3

    S/N analysis

    Averaging each factor's S/N ratio at each level isolates its effect. The level with the highest average S/N is the best choice, and the spread across levels (delta) ranks how much each factor matters.

    Analysis of variance

    ANOVA on the nine run means splits the total variation in Ra between the three factors and error, giving each factor's share and an F-test of significance.
